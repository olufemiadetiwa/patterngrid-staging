The problem

The numbers disagree before the meeting starts.

Sales, stock and finance each keep their own record of the same business. When the systems use different names and different periods, every report begins with an argument about which figure is right, and the decision waits.

How an engagement runs

Connect. Structure. Understand. Automate. Decide.

Five stages. Each ends with something you can check, and each has an owner on your side.

Our approach in full →

  1. Bring information together from the systems your business already uses.

    You receive
    A map of your connected sources
    Owned by
    System owners

  2. Agree one definition for each measure, check the data as it arrives, and hold anything that fails.

    You receive
    Agreed definitions and data model
    Owned by
    An owner for each measure

  3. Show what is happening, what is unusual, and what is driving it.

    You receive
    Reports built around your questions
    Owned by
    The team that uses the report

  4. Reduce repetitive work and route the right information to the right people.

    You receive
    Tested workflows with a named reviewer
    Owned by
    A named reviewer

  5. Put the checked figures, and the evidence behind them, in front of the people making the decision.

    You receive
    A plan for who uses it and how often it is reviewed
    Owned by
    The decision owner
